## What is CN2 GIA, and do you actually need it?

CN2 GIA is China Telecom's premium international backbone, and it exists because ordinary China-bound routes get badly congested. BandwagonHost's own network documentation is unusually blunt about this: standard transit routes can hit 30% packet loss at peak hours, which makes serving mainland visitors — video calls, games, even plain web pages — unreliable. China Telecom stopped peering with China Unicom and China Mobile on China-to-US routes back in 2019, making the congestion problem worse for everyone.

The GIA tier fixes it at a price: transit costs can reach $120 per megabit on this network, which is why GIA plans cost multiples of the general ones. BandwagonHost operates 8×10 Gbe CN2 GIA/CTGNet links across two Los Angeles datacenters, and the USCA_9 (DC9) location routes all China-bound traffic across three carriers — China Telecom CN2 GIA, China Mobile CMIN2, and China Unicom Premium. The decision rule is simple.
